# Customer Discovery

Real customer evidence beats desk research. The agent cannot interview people, so it prepares the user to do it and analyzes the results.

## Workflow

1. **Define learning goals**: the 3 riskiest assumptions about customers (from the Assumption Register).
2. **Interview guide** (problem-focused, no pitching): past behavior questions ("ceritakan terakhir kali kamu..."), current solutions, spending, triggers, and a closing ask for referrals. Avoid hypotheticals and leading questions.
3. **Survey** (optional): max 10 questions, with screening question, willingness-to-pay ladder, and sample size guidance. Note bias risks.
4. **Recruiting plan**: where to find 8-15 target respondents and a simple tracking table.
5. **Synthesis** (after the user pastes notes): Jobs-to-be-Done statements, pains ranked by frequency and intensity, evidence quotes, and personas labeled `data-backed` or `hypothesis`. Update the Assumption Register (confirmed, refuted, unclear).
6. **Save** to `prps/<project>/14-customer-discovery.md`. Checkpoint: ask whether to continue.
