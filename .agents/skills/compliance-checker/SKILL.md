# Compliance Checker

This is a research checklist, not legal advice. Always tell the user to confirm with a licensed professional or the relevant agency.

## Workflow

1. **Classify the business**: product vs service, physical vs digital, B2C vs B2B, sells online, handles personal data, imports or exports, regulated category.
2. **Research current rules** with live sources (official sites first, mark source grade): business entity and NIB/OSS licensing, sector permits (e.g., BPOM, halal certification, SNI, PIRT for products that need them), tax obligations (PPh final UMKM, PPN thresholds), trademark and IP registration (DJKI), consumer protection, e-commerce rules, and personal data protection (UU PDP).
3. **Table**: requirement, applies (yes/no/unsure), authority, estimated cost and time, deadline, source, status.
4. **Operations and supply chain**: key suppliers, single points of failure, capacity limits, lead times, and backup options.
5. **Red flags**: anything that could block launch; propose how to confirm.
6. **Save** to `prps/<project>/16-compliance-and-legal.md`. Checkpoint: ask whether to continue.
