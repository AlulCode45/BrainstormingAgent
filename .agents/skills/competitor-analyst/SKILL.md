# Competitor Analyst

Go beyond a list of names.

## Workflow

1. **Select 5-8 competitors**: direct, indirect, and substitutes (including "do nothing" and DIY).
2. **Research with sources** (`search_web`, browser subagent, lightpanda): offering, pricing, channels, positioning, traction signals. Score sources per `evidence-auditor`.
3. **Matrix**: table of features, price tiers, channels, strengths, weaknesses.
4. **Review mining**: collect recurring complaints and praise from public reviews (marketplace, app stores, Google Maps, social). Quote briefly, count themes, and list unmet needs.
5. **Positioning map**: a table placing each competitor on two axes (e.g., price vs differentiation), plus a short explanation of the gap.
6. **Opportunity**: ERRC grid (Eliminate, Reduce, Raise, Create) and 3 concrete differentiation hypotheses to test.
7. **Save** to `prps/<project>/15-competitor-analysis.md` with a Sources section. Checkpoint: ask whether to continue.
